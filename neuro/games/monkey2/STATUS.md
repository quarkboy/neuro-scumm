# Monkey Island 2: status

Set up on 17 November 2026 as the second game. Nothing here has been reviewed by a person.

## What works
- The game starts without anyone touching the keyboard: the copy protection screen is
  passed, she picks the full game or the short one on the screen that follows, and the
  opening plays. For a stream, a savegame made after that choice skips all of it.
- Rooms, things, dialogue and her actions are reported as in the first game, with the
  game's own names.

Work stopped here on purpose: the folder shows that a second game needs no change to
the module, and the writing below waits until someone means to play this game.

## What is missing
- Descriptions, names and notes for all 110 rooms but the first screen. Until they are
  written she is told "room 5" and a list of the game's own object names.
- Names for the characters the game leaves unnamed ("Stranger 1" and so on).
- Goals, clues, alerts and watched variables: none yet.
- A check of every kind of scene the first game needed special handling for (rooms with
  their own mouse handler, close-ups, maps).
